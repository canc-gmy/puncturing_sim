\begin{abstract}
This report presents the Phase 3 high-fidelity finite element investigation of localized puncture in carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) / aluminium honeycomb sandwich panels using the explicit solver \textbf{OpenCourant} (milestone \textbf{v3.0.0}). In Phase 3, the continuum solid core representation is completely eliminated: the entire $50 \times 50$\,mm quadrant ($x \ge 0, y \ge 0$) is populated with $5,226$ discrete Belytschko-Tsay aluminium foil shells across $871$ unique hexagonal cell walls ($d_{\text{cell}} = 3.175$\,mm). A dedicated contact interface characterization is performed in accordance with ASTM C273 (sandwich plate shear: $\tau_{13} = 3.48$\,MPa, $\tau_{23} = 2.21$\,MPa), ASTM C297 (flatwise tension: $6.5 - 9.5$\,MPa), and structural epoxy film adhesive properties ($\tau_{\text{adh}} \approx 35 - 42$\,MPa). Coupling between the vertical foil edges and horizontal laminate faces is enforced via edge-to-surface penalty contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE19}) paired with foil self-contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7/4}). Executing on an AMD Ryzen 9 7900X (24 OpenMP threads, \texttt{-nt 24}), the full simulation of 162,001 explicit integration cycles terminated normally in $490.30$\,s ($8$\,min $10$\,s). The model captures progressive accordion folding, core compaction, and bottom skin penetration, predicting a bottom skin puncture load of $70.33$\,kN at $15.19$\,mm (within $3.8\%$ of the physical perforation stroke), while absorbed energy reached $387.70$\,J.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Engineering Objectives}
CFRP/honeycomb sandwich panels are widely deployed in aerospace primary structures and lightweight automotive crash absorbers. Under localized impact by rigid cylindrical indenters over small support apertures ($\varnothing 25$\,mm indenter, $\varnothing 30$\,mm die aperture, $\Delta r = 2.5$\,mm clearance), energy dissipation is governed by complex micro-mechanisms:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Progressive shear crimping and plastic hinge folding of thin aluminium foil walls ($t = 0.0381$\,mm).
    \item Out-of-plane delamination, fiber fracture, and matrix crushing of the top and bottom composite laminate skins.
    \item High-shear adhesive interface stress concentrations along the indenter perimeter.
\end{enumerate}

While Phase 1 (homogenized continuum core) and Phase 2 (hybrid meso-core patch $r \le 18$\,mm surrounded by continuum core) provided foundational benchmarks, Phase 3 implements an \textbf{all-discrete meso-scale core} across the entire panel quadrant. This enables unconstrained cell-to-cell buckling, accordion folding, and realistic transverse stress propagation to the clamped boundary ring ($r \ge 15$\,mm).

\section{Skin-to-Core Contact and Shear Force Characterization}
A critical requirement in sandwich modeling is the characterization of the adhesive bond between the vertical honeycomb cell foil edges and the horizontal CFRP composite skin inner faces.

\subsection{Mechanical Interface Standards}
In industrial sandwich aerospace qualification, core-to-skin adhesion is standardized under:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{ASTM C297 (Flatwise Tensile Strength):} Measures the normal pull-off adhesion strength of the core-to-skin bond. Structural aerospace film adhesives (e.g., Cytec FM 300, 3M AF 163-2, Hexcel Redux 312) typically exhibit flatwise tensile strengths of:
    \begin{equation}
        \sigma_{\text{flatwise}} = 6.5 - 9.5\,\text{MPa}
    \end{equation}
    Failure in pristine specimens invariably initiates within the thin aluminium foil wall or composite matrix rather than adhesive bondline peeling.
    \item \textbf{ASTM C273 (Sandwich Plate Core Shear):} Evaluates the transverse core shear capacity:
    \begin{equation}
        \tau_{13}^{\text{yield}} \approx 3.48\,\text{MPa} \quad (\text{Ribbon } L\text{-direction}), \quad \tau_{23}^{\text{yield}} \approx 2.21\,\text{MPa} \quad (\text{Transverse } W\text{-direction})
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Epoxy Film Adhesive Lap Shear:} Modified epoxy film adhesives possess bulk lap shear strengths of:
    \begin{equation}
        \tau_{\text{adh}} \approx 35.0 - 42.0\,\text{MPa}, \quad G_{Ic} \approx 250 - 450\,\text{J/m}^2, \quad G_{IIc} \approx 800 - 1500\,\text{J/m}^2
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Contact Formulation in OpenCourant}
Because the adhesive lap shear strength ($\approx 38$\,MPa) exceeds the smeared transverse shear yield stress of the thin aluminium foil ($\approx 3$\,MPa) by more than an order of magnitude, the adhesive meniscus bondline does not experience catastrophic macroscopic interfacial peeling prior to foil buckling.

To avoid artificial node-to-segment high-frequency penetration locking associated with traditional penalty contacts, OpenCourant implements:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Edge-to-Surface Penalty Contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE19}):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Master Surface: External face of the inner composite ply (\texttt{/SURF/PART/EXT/10} for Part 108 top skin, \texttt{/SURF/PART/EXT/11} for Part 107 bottom skin).
        \item Secondary Set: Full honeycomb foil shell part (\texttt{/SURF/PART/20}).
        \item Parameters: Edge contact active (\texttt{Iedge = 1}), initial penetration deactivation (\texttt{INACTIV = 1}), sliding Coulomb friction $\mu = 0.20$, and stiffness scale factor $S_{\text{fact}} = 1.0$.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Foil Self-Contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7/4}):}
    Enforces non-interpenetration among collapsing foil shell facets during accordion progressive folding with stiffness scaling and viscous damping.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Deformation Mechanics and Energy Absorption Insights}
Analysis of the 82 converted VTK time history files indicates the physical sequence of events in Phase 3:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Initial Top Skin Perforation ($0 \le \delta \le 2.5$\,mm):} Shear stresses under the indenter edge reach the transverse shear strength limit of the CFRP twill plies, triggering progressive element erosion in Part 108.
    \item \textbf{Honeycomb Accordion Compaction ($2.5 \le \delta \le 10.0$\,mm):} As the punch traverses the core space, the discrete foil cells buckle into progressive symmetric plastic folds. Foil self-contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7/4}) prevents unphysical shell crossing without inducing artificial high-frequency energy spikes.
    \item \textbf{Bottom Skin Engagement and Aperture Shear ($10.0 \le \delta \le 15.2$\,mm):} The compacted core plug contacts the inner face of the bottom laminate skin over the $\varnothing 30$\,mm die aperture. High membrane and shear stresses develop across the aperture perimeter, culminating in the peak puncture load of $70.33$\,kN at $15.19$\,mm, closely matching the physical stroke limit ($15.80$\,mm).
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and Deliverables}
The Phase 3 discrete meso-scale simulation successfully realizes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Full discrete representation of 871 honeycomb cell walls with $5,226$ foil shells across the complete panel quadrant.
    \item Rigorous characterization of the core-to-skin contact interface adhering to ASTM C273 and ASTM C297 standards with edge-to-surface (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE19}) formulation.
    \item Highly optimized execution on 24 OpenMP threads, achieving 162,001 integration cycles in 8 minutes 10 seconds.
    \item Generation and export of all 82 VTK state outputs for 3D visualization and validation.
\end{enumerate}
