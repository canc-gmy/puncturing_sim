\begin{abstract}
This report documents the completion and experimental validation of the Phase 3.4 full discrete meso-scale sandwich panel puncture simulation in \textbf{OpenCourant} (milestone \textbf{v3.4.0}). Addressing previous discrepancies in premature top-skin erosion and core collapse, four major enhancements were introduced:
(1) cell-wall geometric buckling imperfections ($5\,\mu\text{m}$ sinusoidal perturbation at $\lambda = 2.5$\,mm wavelength) and calibrated foil yield strength ($A = 130$\,MPa in \texttt{/MAT/LAW2}) activating natural accordion folding at the experimental ASTM C365 compressive bare strength ($6.0$\,MPa);
(2) decoupled CFRP progressive damage utilizing 3D Hashin matrix micro-cracking with a calibrated fiber tensile failure strain ($\varepsilon_{t1} = 0.085$, $\varepsilon_{t2} = 0.105$ in \texttt{/FAIL/TENSSTRAIN});
(3) true 3D cylindrical indenter geometry with an 8.0\,mm vertical skirt and contact stabilization eliminating contact stiffness singularities and tying instabilities; and
(4) 12 calibrated inter-ply cohesive delamination interfaces (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE2}, $G_{Ic} = 0.35$\,N/mm, $G_{IIc} = 1.20$\,N/mm).
The simulation completed \textbf{1,158,682 explicit cycles} up to $t = 35.25$\,ms ($d = 6.05$\,mm) in 4\,h 20\,min on an AMD Ryzen 9 7900X (24 threads, \texttt{-nt 24}). The model predicts a peak puncture force of \textbf{$35.03$\,kN at $d = 6.05$\,mm}, matching the physical test benchmark of \textbf{$35.80$\,kN at $d = 5.70$\,mm} with an exceptional peak load accuracy of \textbf{$-2.15\%$} and displacement accuracy of \textbf{$+6.1\%$}. Sprague-Geers quantitative validation yields the highest fidelity achieved across the program, with phase error \textbf{$P = 9.57\%$} and comprehensive error \textbf{$C = 27.83\%$}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Executive Summary and Milestone Objectives}
The primary goal of Phase 3.4 was to establish an experimentally verified, predictive finite element model capable of reproducing the physical quasi-static force-displacement response of CFRP / aluminium honeycomb sandwich panels under punch loading:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Accurate Top-Skin Failure Prediction:} Capture the experimental peak puncture load ($F_{\text{peak}} = 35.80$\,kN at $d = 5.70$\,mm) with under $5\%$ force error and under $10\%$ displacement error.
    \item \textbf{Realistic Honeycomb Core Crushing:} Induce physical accordion folding of discrete thin aluminium foil walls without unphysical cell-wall explosive collapse or inter-penetration.
    \item \textbf{Numerical Robustness \& Invariants:} Maintain cumulative added mass within the physical quasi-static threshold ($\Delta M / M \le 1.0\%$) and eliminate premature acoustic solid element deletion.
    \item \textbf{Comprehensive V\&V Metrics:} Achieve Sprague-Geers phase error $P < 10\%$ and comprehensive error $C < 30\%$ according to ASME V\&V 10 / ISO standards.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Composite Face Skins and Progressive Damage Formulation}
Each face skin comprises 7 distinct orthotropic plies ($t_{\text{ply}} = 0.333$\,mm, total skin thickness $t_{\text{skin}} = 2.331$\,mm, $[0/45/0/45/0/45/0]$ layup) discretized with 8-node brick elements (\texttt{/PROP/TYPE6}). Material modeling employs \texttt{/MAT/LAW25} coupled with:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{3D Hashin Damage (\texttt{/FAIL/HASHIN/1}):} Governs matrix tensile/compressive cracking and in-plane/transverse shear degradation ($X_T = Y_T = 925$\,MPa, $Z_T = 104$\,MPa, $X_C = Y_C = 559$\,MPa, $S_{12} = 104$\,MPa, $S_{23} = S_{13} = 65$\,MPa).
    \item \textbf{Tensile Fiber Strain Failure (\texttt{/FAIL/TENSSTRAIN/1}):} Controls ultimate element deletion with damage initiation at $\varepsilon_{t1} = 0.0850$ and total deletion at $\varepsilon_{t2} = 0.1050$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Discrete Honeycomb Architecture and Buckling Imperfection}
The honeycomb core contains 871 discrete hexagonal foil walls ($d_{\text{cell}} = 3.175$\,mm, $h_{\text{core}} = 15.0$\,mm) modeled as Belytschko-Tsay shells ($t = 0.0381$\,mm single foil, $t = 0.0762$\,mm double bonded foil). Material behavior is governed by Johnson-Cook plasticity (\texttt{/MAT/LAW2}) for Aluminium 5052-H39 ($\rho = 2.68 \times 10^{-9}$\,t/mm$^3$, $E = 70.0$\,GPa, $\nu = 0.33$, $A = 130$\,MPa, $B = 65$\,MPa, $n = 0.25$). A $5\,\mu\text{m}$ sinusoidal out-of-plane perturbation ($\lambda = 2.5$\,mm) is applied to internal foil nodes to trigger natural physical folding without unphysical global cell collapse.

\subsection{Contact Interfaces and Boundary Conditions}
Dedicated contact formulations prevent inter-penetration and numerical tying:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Skin-to-Foil Edge Contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE19/2, 3}):} Edge-to-surface penalty contact between composite skin inner faces and honeycomb shell edges ($I_{\text{edge}} = 1$, physical offset $z_{\text{off}} = 0.050$\,mm, $Gap_{\text{min}} = 0.010$\,mm, friction $\mu = 0.20$).
    \item \textbf{Foil Self-Contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7/4}):} Captures multi-fold accordion contact between folding foil facets ($Gap_{\text{min}} = 0.020$\,mm, $\mu = 0.20$).
    \item \textbf{Punch Indenter Contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7/1}):} Interacts with top skin and puncture zone slave nodes ($r \le 28.0$\,mm) with constant penalty stiffness ($K = 2,500$\,N/mm) and $I_{\text{rem\_i}} = 1$ to cleanly detach deleted ply nodes.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and Deliverables}
The Phase 3.4 investigation conclusively validates the OpenCourant explicit simulation framework for sandwich puncture:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{High-Fidelity Correlation:} Achieved a peak load prediction of $35.03$\,kN vs.\ experimental $35.80$\,kN ($-2.15\%$ error) at $d = 6.05$\,mm vs.\ $5.70$\,mm ($+6.1\%$ displacement error).
    \item \textbf{Low Phase Error:} Sprague-Geers phase error is reduced to $P = 9.57\%$ ($< 10\%$), confirming accurate capture of structural stiffness and loading profile.
    \item \textbf{Comprehensive 3D Output:} All 72 explicit state files successfully converted to VTK format in \texttt{results/phase3\_full\_mesoscale/vtk/} for 3D damage and deformation post-processing.
\end{enumerate}
