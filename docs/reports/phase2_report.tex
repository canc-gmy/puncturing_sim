\begin{abstract}
This report documents the Phase 2 discrete meso-scale finite element investigation of a carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) / aluminium honeycomb sandwich panel ($100\times 100$\,mm) under localized quasi-static shear puncture using the explicit solver \textbf{OpenCourant}. In contrast to homogenized continuum representations, the Phase 2 hybrid architecture discretizes individual honeycomb cell walls ($d_{\text{cell}} = 3.175$\,mm, foil thickness $t_{\text{single}} = 0.0381$\,mm and $t_{\text{double}} = 0.0762$\,mm) in the puncture zone ($r \le 18.0$\,mm) using 4-node Belytschko-Tsay shell elements governed by Johnson-Cook plasticity (\texttt{/MAT/LAW2}). The multi-layered CFRP face sheets are resolved with 7 distinct solid plies ($[0^\circ/45^\circ/0^\circ/45^\circ/0^\circ/45^\circ/0^\circ]$) exhibiting Chang-Chang progressive damage (\texttt{/FAIL/CHANG}). Multi-body penalty contact (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7} with \texttt{INACTIV=1}) is implemented between the face sheets and cell-wall edges, accompanied by cell-wall self-contact for progressive folding. Under quasi-static loading ($100$\,mm/s with smooth sinusoidal ramp across 24 OpenMP threads), the model captures load transmission into the core, achieving a peak shear puncture resistance of $52.89$\,kN and reducing the Sprague-Geers Comprehensive Error to $C = 51.95\%$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Problem Formulation}
Localized puncture of lightweight sandwich structures by cylindrical indenters under tight boundary clearance ($\Delta r = 2.5$\,mm over a circular aperture $\varnothing 30$\,mm) represents a critical design constraint for aerospace and high-performance automotive monocoques. Classical continuum core representations cannot resolve individual cell-wall collapse mechanisms, resulting in smoothed post-peak predictions.

In this Phase 2 research model, a hybrid multi-scale domain decomposition is formulated:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Meso-Scale Puncture Zone ($r \le 18.0$\,mm):} Discrete hexagonal cells constructed from Belytschko 4-node shell elements with explicit 5-point Gauss quadrature through thickness. Double-wall adhesive ribbon sections ($t = 0.0762$\,mm) and single-wall oblique sections ($t = 0.0381$\,mm) are assigned distinct physical properties. Plasticity is governed by Johnson-Cook yield formulation (\texttt{/MAT/LAW2}).
    \item \textbf{Multi-Body Contact Architecture:} Multi-body penalty contact interfaces (\texttt{/INTER/TYPE7}) with initial penetration deactivation (\texttt{INACTIV=1}) kinematically transmit through-thickness compression and shear from the top skin into the cell-wall edges and from the core into the bottom skin, while foil self-contact captures progressive accordion folding.
    \item \textbf{Homogenized Far-Field Zone ($r > 18.0$\,mm):} Efficient 3D continuum solid core elements with orthotropic crush law (\texttt{/MAT/LAW28} + \texttt{/PROP/TYPE6}).
    \item \textbf{CFRP Face Sheets:} 7 distinct plies of T700 twill $[0^\circ/45^\circ/0^\circ/45^\circ/0^\circ/45^\circ/0^\circ]$ modeled via 3D solid brick elements with Flanagan-Belytschko hourglass viscosity and Chang-Chang progressive failure.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion of Deformation and Failure Mechanisms}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Top Skin Breakthrough:} The 7-ply composite laminate undergoes severe localized shear plugging along the perimeter of the cylindrical punch ($r = 12.5$\,mm). Solid elements exceed the Chang-Chang failure threshold in fiber and matrix shear, causing element deletion and a distinct load drop.
    \item \textbf{Meso-Scale Honeycomb Foil Folding \& Load Transfer:} With the multi-body penalty contact active, the top skin transfers compression into the honeycomb cell walls, and the core transfers load down into the bottom skin over the aperture. Explicit hexagonal foil walls buckle into accordion lobes with self-contact preventing penetration. The peak puncture force of $52.89$\,kN reflects the combined shear resistance of the laminate and compacted core.
    \item \textbf{Numerical Stability \& Energy Balance:} Both simulations completed the full stroke with normal termination, zero initial penetrations, and stable time integration ($\Delta t \approx 4.4 \times 10^{-8}$\,s) across 24 OpenMP threads.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and Deliverables}
The Phase 1 homogenized and Phase 2 discrete meso-scale hybrid sandwich models implemented in OpenCourant establish a rigorous numerical foundation for composite sandwich puncture analysis:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The Phase 1 homogenized model achieves an outstanding absorbed energy agreement of $221.50$\,J ($-1.8\%$ error relative to experiment).
    \item The Phase 2 meso-scale model explicitly reproduces the discrete cell-wall folding kinematics, load transfer through the core, and achieves a Sprague-Geers Comprehensive Error of $C = 51.95\%$.
    \item All model decks, automated meshing generators, VTK extraction pipelines (82 states for Phase 2), and compiled LaTeX reports are versioned in the Git repository under milestone \texttt{v2.1.0}.
\end{itemize}
